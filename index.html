What this application is

This is a private, personal-use tool. It is not distributed, not published to any app store, and not offered to the public. It runs entirely on the owner's own computer and is used by exactly one person — the owner of the Google account it is authorized against.

本应用为个人自用工具，不对外发布、不分发、不提供任何公共服务， 仅在本机运行，且仅由 Google 账号本人使用。

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail messages, threads, labels and drafts, and can send mail on the owner's behalf.
  • Reads and manages the owner's Google Contacts.
  • Exposes these operations to a local AI assistant through the Model Context Protocol.

功能：读取本人 Gmail 邮件/标签/草稿、代本人发送邮件、读写本人 Google 通讯录， 并通过本地 MCP 协议提供给本机 AI 助手调用。

Where your data goes

Nowhere. There is no server component, no cloud storage, and no third-party analytics. OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored only in files on the owner's own machine. See the Privacy Policy for details.

数据不出本机：本应用无服务端、无云存储、无第三方统计。令牌与缓存仅存于本机文件。
